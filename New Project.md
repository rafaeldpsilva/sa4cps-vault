## Origin and Client Engagement Process 

The service was originated from a challenge launched by Digitalmente aiming the issue of smart meter data collection and (near-)real-time communication tracking to overcome the current issues associated with remote metering. 

The objective of this intervention was to study the issue, test new methodologies for smart metering network monitoring and tracking and propose an effective means of data collection monitoring and tracking. 

The study will enable a better understanding of the smart metering communication issues and open the opportunity to the network (re)configuration using the existing infrastructure and possible new infrastructure to be added. 

## Challenges Identified 

Smart metering technologies use autoconfigured mesh networks hard to maintain and monitor using third-party software, such as the one developed and maintained by Digitalmente. 

Issues from energy metering demand Digitalmente’s clients to go physically to the location in order to record the energy data to enable the correct monthly energy billing. 

The issues raised from smart meter communication can be generated from several factors. One of special interest in the stability and quality of the energy grid. As smart meter data flows using power line communications, the quality of the energy influences the communication but also the gird itself, being a critical topic for analysis. 

## Description of the Use Case / Implemented Solution 

Current deployment assessment: detailed analysis of the current context to describe exactly what the current situation is. 

Detailed study on technology: a detailed study of the used technologies and the equipment used for the current deployment to identify possible issues and corrections. 

Exploration monitoring and tracking: development of multiple methods for data monitoring and tracking to survey the current infrastructure to assess and identify issues in communication and in the grid (e.g., energy quality). 

Proposal of new methods and models: suitable methods and models will be proposed and discussed with Digitalmente to validate their adoption and deployment in a (near-)real-time environment to enable the correct monitoring and tracking of communications and grid issues. 

Deployment of new methods and models: proof of concept methods and models will be tested. 

Detailed report with the findings: a detailed report will be elaborated to describe all the findings and developments made, with the final evaluation of the corrective actions needed and the investment needed, per issue identified. 

## Implementation 

To address the identified challenge, multiple domains needed to be involved, namely artificial intelligence, internet of things, data science and big data, and cyber-physical systems. The combination of these domains is required to properly address the challenge, enabling the study and proposal of a complete solution suitable for Digitalmente requirements. Being this a study, not all the identified innovative methods and models will be tested due to time limitation and resource limitation; however, all possibilities will be analyzed for a future investment.
