## **Data-Driven Science and Engineering**

- **Authors:** Steven L. Brunton & J. Nathan Kutz (2nd Ed. / 2022-2024 updates)
    
- **Difficulty:** **High.** Requires a background in dynamical systems and linear algebra.
    
- **Vibe:** **The Lab Bible.** It covers SVD, Koopman theory, and model reduction.
    
- **Why read:** If you are trying to use AI to control a microgrid or optimize a turbine, this book teaches you how to combine classical control theory with modern ML. It’s the antidote to the "black box" approach.
