## 1.1       Critical energy distribution (UC4)

<The purpose of this section is to describe the functional areas/blocks into which the use case is contributing, explanation of the colored marks in the figure 7. {I provided the blocks of the use case 4 as "contribution for ..."}>

A distribution network operated by an energy community aggregator, as a cooperative, wants to develop the means to use flexibility in case of reliability issues in the distribution grid or when asked by the upstream network operator. At the same time, this operator is managing an energy community composed of a part of the consumers in the area, targeting to optimize the energy contracts definition considering user’s flexibility, energy prices, and operation costs. A flexibility aggregator gets involved to collect and provide this flexibility.

Main contribution area to the Sa4CPs concept (see Figure 1): Distributed AI/explainable AI, digital twin. Several initiatives in the field focus on energy aspect, ignoring the learning of user's comfort and flexibility.

Targeted innovation boosted by the Sa4CPS concept: Use of Explainable AI (XAI) for situational awareness;
Activation of Flexibility with XAI;
Use of federated learning models for energy communities Target business impact: Use of flexibility;
Optimization of resource use.
