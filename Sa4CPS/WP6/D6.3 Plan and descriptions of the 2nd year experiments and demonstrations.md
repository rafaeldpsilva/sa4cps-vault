Task 6.3 UC 4 - Critical electricity distribution grid operation in energy communities

## 5. Use Case Description

A distribution network operated by DSO, wants to develop the means to use flexibility in case of reliability issues in the distribution grid or when asked by the upstream network operator. At the same time, this operator is managing an energy community composed of a part of the consumers in the area, targeting to optimize the energy contracts definition considering user's flexibility, energy prices, and operation costs. A flexibility aggregator gets involved to collect and provide this flexibility.

Targeted innovation boosted by the Sa4CPS concept: Use of Explainable AI (XAI) for situational awareness; Activation of Flexibility with XAI; Use of federated learning models for energy communities.

### 4. Discussion and estimation of the targeted KPI

The 2nd year experiments and demonstrations target the following key performance indicators:

### 5.3 Demonstration 1 < UC4 — Edge-Based Energy Community Monitoring, Forecasting, and Explainable Situational Awareness>

This integrated UC4 demonstration will present the contributions of CEL, DIGITALMENTE, ENERIM, and ISEP as a single unified energy community scenario. CEL will provide field consumer access and metering infrastructure from real buildings. DIGITALMENTE will supply smart metering data and support integration with existing data systems. ENERIM will provide consumer energy data via REST API interfaces. ISEP will deploy the distributed edge infrastructure, operate the federated learning and XAI pipelines, and manage the community-level graphical interfaces.

The demonstration will show how IoT sensor data collected at building-level edge nodes can be transformed into actionable energy community situational awareness. The full data path will be traced from smart meters and environmental sensors publishing to local MQTT brokers on Raspberry Pi 5 nodes, through the data pipeline feeding digital twin and dashboard services, to federated learning-trained consumption forecasts and XAI-generated explanations rendered as daily summaries and interactive visualizations.

Desk-level energy monitors deployed in target buildings will display real-time energy metrics to occupants, demonstrating the local awareness dimension. At the community level, the dashboard will present aggregated views including import/export energy balances, where individual building contributions are explained in the context of community-level energy balance through XAI-generated summaries. Both real edge hardware and the virtual energy community simulation will run simultaneously, with virtual households extending the demonstration to community scale.

The explainable AI dimension will be linked to the forecasting outputs. XAI techniques applied to the federated learning models will produce daily summaries that explain key consumption patterns, highlight anomalies, and describe the factors driving flexibility estimates. The demonstration will show how raw model outputs are transformed into comprehensible, actionable information for non-technical community managers and building occupants.

Key functionalities to be demonstrated:

- Real-time IoT data collection from smart meters, environmental sensors, and smart plugs via distributed MQTT brokers on Raspberry Pi 5 edge nodes
- End-to-end data pipeline from sensor reading to platform service consumption
- Desk-level energy monitors displaying real-time building consumption to occupants
- Federated learning-trained energy consumption forecasts produced without sharing raw household data across nodes
- Privacy-preserving model training across real households and virtual community simulation households
- Community dashboard with aggregated energy views
- XAI-generated daily summaries explaining system outputs
- Interactive visualizations transforming model outputs into actionable comprehension for non-technical users
- VPN overlay enabling secure inter-building communication across distributed nodes
- Consent-aware data sharing mechanisms governing building data exposure to the community

**Partner contributions:** CEL will select field consumers, provide access to metering infrastructure, and facilitate access to community managers for live feedback during the demonstration. DIGITALMENTE will provide smart metering data access and support integration of data presentation and XAI outputs into existing systems. ENERIM will provide consumer profile data via REST API interfaces to support model personalization. ISEP will deploy the edge infrastructure, configure the data pipeline, run the federated learning models, operate the XAI pipeline, generate explanations, develop desk monitor interfaces, and manage the community graphical interfaces.

**Demonstration type:** Live demonstration with real edge hardware and virtual energy community simulation running in parallel, combined with pre-recorded XAI explanation outputs to ensure demonstration reliability.
