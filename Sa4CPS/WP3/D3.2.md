## Critical Electricity Distribution Grid Operation in Energy Communities (UC 4)

### 3.4.1 Perception Enablers

---

#### P_EN_UC4.1 — Autonomous Detection and Mitigation of Voltage Issues

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.10  
**Author:** ISEP

**Description:** Focus on the situational awareness frameworks capable of distinguishing between nominal grid states and emergency conditions.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Anomaly detection algorithms and monitoring systems

**Relevance:** Disaster prevention, mitigation and discovery of anomalies in the distribution network.

---

#### P_EN_UC4.2 — Secure Data Ingestion & Validation Layer

**Type:** System / Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.3, REQ_UC4.4, REQ_UC4.10  
**Author:** Digitalmente

**Description:** Secure perception layer responsible for ingesting, validating, and authenticating IoT and metering data originating from distributed energy resources, consumer premises, and grid assets.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Message integrity verification and timestamp validation
- Detection of missing, duplicated, or anomalous telemetry
- Edge/fog deployment to minimize data exposure

**Relevance:** Ensures that grid and consumption data used for situational awareness and flexibility activation is trustworthy, tamper-resistant, and compliant with critical infrastructure security expectations.

---

#### P_EN_UC4.3 — Consent-Aware Energy Data Perception Module

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.6, REQ_UC4.7, REQ_UC4.8  
**Author:** Digitalmente, CEL

**Description:** Perception module that filters and contextualizes incoming energy data based on explicit consumer consent and role-based access rules, ensuring GDPR-compliant data acquisition for energy community operations.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Consent registry linked to consumer identities
- Attribute-level data filtering
- Role-based data exposure (consumer / CEL operator / DSO)
- Audit logging of data access and usage

**Relevance:** Allows CEL to perceive only authorized data while maintaining consumer trust and regulatory compliance.

---

### 3.4.2 Comprehension Enablers

---

#### C_EN_UC4.1 — Digital Twin for Electricity Distribution Network with Distributed Generation and Flexible Load

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.1, REQ_UC4.2  
**Author:** ISEP

**Description:** Digital twin for the electricity distribution network serves as the foundational analytical engine, utilizing state estimation algorithms and historical baseline datasets. It synthesizes virtual sensor data to maintain operational continuity in the event of telemetry loss.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- State prediction

**Relevance:** Ensure that, in case of failure of communication, data loss is minimized.

---

#### C_EN_UC4.2 — Dashboard for Energy Community Management

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.7, REQ_UC4.13, REQ_UC4.14  
**Author:** ISEP

**Description:** UI and a series of REST API calls designed to handle the lifecycle of community participants and allow operators to monitor production and consumption trends at both the prosumer and community levels.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Permissioned data for consumers
- Automated reporting and tracking of KPI analytics

**Relevance:** Providing transparency for the users, encouraging participation in the community. Also enables management by the DSO.

---

#### C_EN_UC4.3 — Activation of Flexible Resources

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.12  
**Author:** ISEP

**Description:** Real-time actuation through the activation of flexible resources, specifically targeting battery energy storage systems and electric vehicle integration.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Dynamic response constraints to ensure requirements are met
- Robust communication with deployed assets

**Relevance:** Enables the actuation in cases of emergencies, voltage issues or overloads.

---

#### C_EN_UC4.4 — Energy Community Situational Comprehension Engine

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.1, REQ_UC4.11, REQ_UC4.13, REQ_UC4.17  
**Author:** Digitalmente, CEL, ISEP

**Description:** Transforms perceived grid, consumption, and flexibility data into actionable situational understanding for energy community operators. It provides aggregated views, trend analysis, and early warnings for abnormal or inefficient states.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Aggregation of consumption, production, and flexibility signals
- KPI computation (self-consumption, peak reduction, emissions)
- Detection of abnormal community behavior
- Visualization tailored to CEL operational workflows
- Support for scenario comparison and historical analysis

**Relevance:** Enables CEL operators to understand the current and projected state of the energy community and act proactively.

---

#### C_EN_UC4.5 — Trust & Data Quality Assessment Engine

**Type:** Software  
**Supported Requirement (D3.1):** REQ_UC4.1, REQ_UC4.3  
**Author:** Digitalmente

**Description:** Reasoning component that continuously evaluates the trustworthiness, freshness, and completeness of energy data streams and digital twin states, assigning confidence levels to situational outputs.

**Technical Characteristics:**
- Data quality scoring (freshness, completeness, consistency)
- Confidence indicators for operators
- Integration with digital twin state estimation
- Fallback logic in case of telemetry loss
- Explainable reasoning outputs

**Relevance:** Prevents incorrect decisions caused by missing or unreliable data and strengthens operator confidence in automated recommendations.
