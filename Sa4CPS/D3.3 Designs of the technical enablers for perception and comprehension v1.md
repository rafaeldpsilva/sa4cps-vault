#### _**Autonomous detection and mitigation of voltage issues**_ _**(ISEP)**_

- **Purpose of the perception mechanism**: To serve as a situational awareness framework capable of monitoring the distribution network continuously to distinguish between nominal grid states and emergency conditions, preventing disasters and discovering anomalies (e.g., voltage issues or overloads).
- **Sensors, devices, or data sources**: Smart meters, grid sensors (measuring voltage, current, and frequency), and edge monitoring systems deployed across the distribution network.
- **System architecture and main components**: Comprises edge monitoring systems located near the data sources, connected to a centralized or decentralized anomaly detection processing framework.
- **Data collection and transmission processes**: Continuous streaming or periodic sampling of electrical measurements from edge devices and meters to the central system.
- **Communication technologies and protocols**: Industrial and IoT protocols suited for reliable telemetry (e.g., MQTT, Modbus TCP) ensuring real-time data transmission.
- **Security and data integrity mechanisms**: Secure communication channels with data encryption (e.g., TLS/SSL) to protect telemetry data from tampering.
- **Types of data generated**: Streams of raw and pre-processed electrical measurements, alongside preliminary anomaly flags or alerts from edge algorithms, which are forwarded to the comprehension layer for deeper analysis and mitigation strategy generation.

#### _**Digital twin for electricity distribution network with distributed generation and flexible load**_ _**(**__**ISEP**__**)**_

- **Purpose of the comprehension mechanism**: Serves as the foundational analytical engine to maintain operational continuity and minimize data loss in the event of communication failures.
- **Data integration**: Ingests real-time telemetry from grid sensors, smart meters, and historical baseline datasets from the perception layer.
- **Data processing methods**: Utilizes state estimation algorithms and state prediction models to synthesize missing information.
- **System architecture and data processing pipeline**: A data ingestion pipeline feeding into a state estimation engine, which updates a continuous digital representation (the Digital Twin) of the physical network.
- **Outputs produced by the system**: Synthesized virtual sensor data, predicted grid states, and a continuous, reliable digital representation of the network.
- **Prediction mechanisms**: State prediction algorithms to synthesize virtual sensor data when physical telemetry is lost or degraded.
- **Operational awareness support**: Provides operators with a reliable, uninterrupted view of the grid state even during communication outages, ensuring situational awareness is maintained at all times.

#### _**Dashboard for energy community management**_ _**(ISEP)**_

- **Purpose of the comprehension mechanism**: To handle the lifecycle of community participants, provide transparency for users to encourage participation, and enable comprehensive management by the DSO.
- **Data integration**: Consolidates permissioned data regarding consumers, production, consumption, and system analytics from the perception layer.
- **Data processing methods**: Data aggregation, automated reporting generation, and calculation of KPI analytics.
- **System architecture and data processing pipeline**: A backend processing engine exposing a series of REST API calls that feed aggregated data to a frontend User Interface (UI).
- **Outputs produced by the system**: Visual dashboards, automated reports, and continuous tracking of production and consumption trends at both prosumer and community levels.
- **Optimisation mechanisms**: Tracking and visualizing trends to provide insights that support community performance optimization.
- **Operational awareness support**: Gives operators and the DSO a clear, user-friendly visual overview of the community's state, fostering transparency and facilitating informed lifecycle management decisions.

#### _**Activation of flexible resources**_ _**(ISEP)**_

- **Purpose of the comprehension mechanism**: To enable real-time actuation of flexible resources (specifically targeting battery energy storage systems and electric vehicle integration) in cases of emergencies, voltage issues, or overloads.
- **Data integration**: Processes alerts, grid state data, and flexibility availability information to determine necessary actuation responses.
- **Data processing methods**: Optimization and control algorithms that evaluate dynamic response constraints to ensure actuation requirements are met.
- **System architecture and data processing pipeline**: A control decision engine that receives situational alerts, calculates required flexibility, and issues commands via robust communication interfaces to deployed assets.
- **Outputs produced by the system**: Real-time actuation commands and dispatch signals sent directly to flexible resources.
- **Optimisation mechanisms**: Evaluates dynamic response constraints to ensure mitigation strategies successfully and safely resolve detected grid issues.
- **Operational awareness support**: Closes the loop between awareness and control by translating situational comprehension into actionable mitigation steps, actively stabilizing the grid based on the perceived state.

#### _**Energy Community Situational Comprehension Engine**_ _**(Digitalmente, CEL, ISEP)**_

- **Purpose of the comprehension mechanism**: Transforms perceived grid, consumption, and flexibility data into actionable situational understanding for energy community (CEL) operators to act proactively.
- **Data integration**: Aggregates consumption, production, and flexibility signals from the perception layer.
- **Data processing methods**: Trend analysis, scenario comparison, historical analysis, and KPI computation (e.g., self-consumption rates, peak reduction, emissions).
- **System architecture and data processing pipeline**: An ingestion and processing engine that aggregates signals, computes metrics, and feeds into visualization tools tailored to CEL operational workflows.
- **Outputs produced by the system**: Actionable insights, aggregated views, KPI reports, and early warnings for abnormal or inefficient states.
- **Anomaly detection mechanisms**: Specialized algorithms for the detection of abnormal community behavior and inefficient operational states.
- **Operational awareness support**: Empowers CEL operators with a comprehensive, current, and projected view of the energy community, supporting proactive decision-making, operational planning, and scenario evaluation.
