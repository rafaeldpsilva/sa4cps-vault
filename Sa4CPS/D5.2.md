3.3 Trust, security and privacy in the UC4: Critical electricity distribution grid operation in energy communities The UC4 focuses on the real-time monitoring and actuation of electrical loads within an energy community. Given the nature of the problem, the technical enablers must prevent unauthorized access to the data or the system. To ensure this, control signals must be verified and ensured they come from a reliable source and has not been modified in a harmful way. The monitoring system must provide a continuous data stream to prevent “blind” grid management. Also, the system should implement an authentication comp onent, such as zero trust mechanism. Regarding privacy concerns, and as the monitoring of electrical loads may reveal sensitive information due to the high frequency of the monitoring, measures such only data necessary for the operation of the grid should be transmitted to the central controller, and data should be anonymized and encrypted. Additionally, the system should incorporate mechanisms of transparency, such as verifiability and ways to certify and trust certain devices.
