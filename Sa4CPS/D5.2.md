## 3.3 Trust, Security and Privacy in UC4: Critical Electricity Distribution Grid Operation in Energy Communities

The UC4 focuses on the real-time monitoring and actuation of electrical loads within an energy community. Given the nature of the problem, the technical enablers must prevent unauthorized access to the data or the system. To ensure this, control signals must be verified and ensured they come from a reliable source and have not been modified in a harmful way. The monitoring system must provide a continuous data stream to prevent "blind" grid management. Also, the system should implement an authentication component, such as a zero trust mechanism. Regarding privacy concerns, and as the monitoring of electrical loads may reveal sensitive information due to the high frequency of the monitoring, measures such that only data necessary for the operation of the grid should be transmitted to the central controller, and data should be anonymized and encrypted. Additionally, the system should incorporate mechanisms of transparency, such as verifiability and ways to certify and trust certain devices.

**Description:** This enabler centralizes user and entity management. It ensures that only authorized DSO (Distribution System Operator) personnel or community managers can actuate loads or access granular consumption data. ### Data Encryption (UC4)

**Description:** Encrypting data in transit is a fundamental security measure to protect sensitive information and maintain the trust and integrity of communication systems:

- **Confidentiality:** Encryption ensures that sensitive data remains confidential while being transmitted over networks. Unauthorized parties cannot read the data without the decryption key.
- **Integrity:** Encryption helps protect data from being altered or tampered with during transmission. It ensures that the data received is the same as the data sent.
- **Authentication:** Encryption can help verify the identity of the parties involved in the communication, ensuring that data is being sent and received by the intended parties.
- **Compliance:** Many industries have regulations and standards (such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS) that require encryption of data in transit to protect sensitive information.
- **Protection Against Attacks:** Encryption helps protect against various types of cyber attacks, such as man-in-the-middle attacks, eavesdropping, and data breaches.

We will enhance the security of EnerimCIS by encrypting data in transit between EnerimCIS UI and Facade.Main backend.
